A Comparative Study of Restaurant Review Sentiment Classification Across Thai Review Platforms

This project studies sentiment classification of Thai restaurant reviews from two different review platforms: Wongnai and Google Maps. We compare traditional machine learning models and different class-imbalance handling strategies to determine which approach performs best across different review platforms.

The question

Two Platforms. One Complex Signal.

Online restaurant reviews hold a detailed record of customer satisfaction. Yet the signal is rarely balanced: some ratings dominate, while the quiet minority is hardest for a model to recognize.

Review language also shifts between platforms. Wongnai and Google Maps carry different writing styles, rating habits, and review contexts. This project asks whether the way we handle class imbalance changes what a model can learn—and how well those lessons travel.

Research background

Related Work

Previous studies examine Thai restaurant sentiment classification (Songram et al., 2025), Google Maps restaurant reviews (Shin et al., 2022), and cross-domain Thai sentiment classification (Boonpetch & Chitsobhuk, 2019). Research on hybrid WangchanBERTa models (Songram et al., 2026) and SMOTE (Chawla et al., 2002) also informs our approach. Building on these studies, we compare three classifiers and class-imbalance strategies across Wongnai and Google Maps.
